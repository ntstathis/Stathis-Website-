Special equipment for transporting hanging meats:

- Reinforcement of the roof for the transport of hanging meats
- Italian-style meat system consisting of anodized aluminum rails, suitable also for the hooks’ collection
- The aluminum rails are fixed to the roof of the vehicle with stainless brackets
- Special constructions with fixed stainless steel bars on the side walls of the loading area and on the back wall
