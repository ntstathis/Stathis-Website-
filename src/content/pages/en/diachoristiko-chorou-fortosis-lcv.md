#### Simultaneous transport of products at different temperatures

- Fixed van partition
- Movable and removable partition panel

The partition panel can be equipped with fans to maintain a different cooling temperature in the two sections of the cargo area. The temperature of each section is adjusted using an electronic temperature controller located in the driver’s cabin

For the simultaneous transport of products in refrigerated and dry cargo temperatures, the partition panel installed does not have fans.
