The **KOALA 600** is an isothermal refrigerated container designed to ensure the transport of temperature-controlled products. With a 600-liter capacity, a front-opening design, and ATP certification, it meets international standards for the transport of perishable goods. Its dimensions and capacity are optimized to make the most efficient use of loading space in utility vehicles. Compliant with FD X 15-140 standards, it is the ideal solution for professional transport needs.

- **Capacity**: 600 liters
- **Material**: Durable and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Certifications**: ATP certified and compliant with FD X 15-140 standards
- **Practical design**: Front-opening door for easy product access
- **Space optimization**: Dimensions specifically designed to maximize loading space in utility vehicles
