The **KOALA 370** is an isothermal refrigerated container with a capacity of 370 liters. Made from durable polyethylene and insulated with eco-friendly polyurethane foam free of CFCs and HCFCs, it combines high thermal performance with sustainable design. ATP-certified, it meets international standards for the transport of perishable goods. It is available in versions with a cooling unit mounted either on the top or at the rear, offering maximum operational flexibility.

- **Capacity**: 370 liters
- **Material**: Durable and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Certification**: Compliant with standards for the transport of perishable goods (ATP certified)
- **Practical design**: Front-opening door for easy access to contents
- **Available options**: Versions with the cooling unit mounted on the top or at the rear
