The **KOALA 140** is an isothermal refrigerated container designed for the transport of temperature-controlled products. Its stackable design makes it practical and ideal for optimizing space during transport and storage.

- **Capacity**: 135 liters
- **Material**: Durable and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Practical design**: Front-opening door for quick and easy product access
- **Modularity**: Stackable for efficient transport and storage
- **Internal dimensions**: 600×400 mm
