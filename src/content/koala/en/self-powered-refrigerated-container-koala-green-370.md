A battery-powered mobile refrigerated box offers an efficient and flexible solution for professional transport needs. Thanks to its integrated battery, the **KOALA GREEN 140** provides autonomous operation without the need for continuous external power. The battery can be easily recharged by connecting the unit to a 230Vac power supply or by removing it from the cooling module and using a dedicated external charger. Its stackable design makes the KOALA GREEN 140 practical and ideal for space optimization during transport and storage.

- **Capacity**: 135 liters
- **Material**: Robust and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Autonomous power supply**: Via 230Vac or external charger
- **Certification**: ATP-certified for the transport of perishable goods
- **Practical design**: Front-opening door for easy product access
- **Stackability**: Structure designed to optimize space during transport and storage
