The **Koala 1300 TW** is the ideal isothermal refrigerated container for the transport of temperature-controlled products.

- **Extra-large capacity**: With a volume of 1,115 liters, it offers ample space for transporting large quantities of goods while consistently maintaining the ideal temperature
- **High-quality materials**: Made from durable polyethylene and insulated with eco-friendly polyurethane foam free of CFCs and HCFCs, it ensures excellent thermal insulation, preserves the organoleptic properties of food, and reduces the risk of bacterial growth
- **Optimized dimensions**: External dimensions comply with ASIAN PALLET standards (110×110 cm), making pallet loading and unloading extremely practical and efficient, streamlining logistics processes
- **Front-opening design**: Allows quick and easy access to contents, enhancing efficiency during handling operations
- **Global flexibility**: Ideal for international transport, the Koala 1300 TW is the perfect choice for logistics needs across Asian and U.S. markets
