**Compact mobile refrigerator**, ideal for the safe transport of temperature-sensitive products.

- **Capacity:** 21 liters, perfect for small-scale or specialized transport needs.
- **Integrated handles:** Conveniently built-in handles for easy and secure carrying.
- **Internal protection:** Equipped with a protective internal mesh for the evaporator, made of metal wire with plastic coating, ensuring durability and safety.
- **Dual power supply:** Compatible with both 12V DC and 230V AC power sources, offering maximum flexibility for both mobile and stationary use.
- **Built-in cooling unit:** A static system providing efficient and uniform cooling.
