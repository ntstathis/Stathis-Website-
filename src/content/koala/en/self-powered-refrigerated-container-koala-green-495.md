The **KOALA GREEN 495** is a self-contained refrigerated insulated container, designed for the safe transport of perishable goods under controlled temperatures. Thanks to its integrated rechargeable battery, it ensures autonomous operation.

- **Internal capacity**: 495 liters, ideal for transporting fresh and frozen products.
- **ATP certified**, in accordance with international standards for the transport of perishable goods.
- **Durable material**: Polyethylene structure with high insulation, built for intensive use.
- **Thermal insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam, free of CFCs and HCFCs, ensuring excellent temperature retention.
- **Autonomous power supply**: Built-in rechargeable battery that allows independent operation without a continuous power source.
- **Flexible charging**: Can be charged via 230V AC or with an external power adapter, offering versatile usage.
- **Integrated cooling unit**: Provides precise and reliable temperature control, maintaining optimal storage conditions.
- **Optimized internal dimensions (600×400 mm)**: Ideal for boxes and trays, maximizing loading capacity and operational efficiency.
