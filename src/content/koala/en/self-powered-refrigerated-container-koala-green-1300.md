The **Koala Green 1300** refrigerated container is a self-powered isothermal cooling unit, equipped with an integrated battery that ensures autonomous operation. The battery can be recharged either by connecting the container to a standard 230Vac power supply or by removing it from the cooling unit to recharge it using a special external power adapter.

- **Capacity**: 1115 liters, with a front-opening door for convenient and quick access.
- **Construction materials**: Made of polyethylene and insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam, providing excellent thermal insulation.
- **Dimensions**: Compatible with the EUROPALLET standard (80×120 cm), allowing for optimal space management and perfect fit in commercial vehicles.
