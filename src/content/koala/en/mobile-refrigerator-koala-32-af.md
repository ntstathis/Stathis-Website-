Ideal for the safe and reliable transport of temperature-sensitive products, the **Koala 32** combines efficiency, durability, and practicality in a compact and functional design.

- **Optimal capacity**: 32 liters, perfect for transporting small to medium quantities of temperature-sensitive goods
- **High-quality materials**: Made from durable polyethylene for long-lasting protection, with eco-friendly polyurethane foam insulation (CFC- and HCFC-free) that ensures excellent thermal retention
- **Dual power supply**: Compatible with both 12Vdc and 230Vac, allowing flexible use on the go or in stationary settings
- **Functional design**: Front-opening for easy and quick access, stackable for space optimization during transport or storage
- **Ease of use**: Equipped with integrated handles for safe and comfortable carrying
