The **KOALA 500** is a refrigerated insulated container designed for the safe and reliable transport of temperature-controlled products, ensuring maximum thermal efficiency and flexibility.

- Version with top-mounted cooling unit (roof-mounted refrigeration)
- Version with rear-mounted cooling unit (rear refrigeration system)

- **Internal capacity**: 500 liters, ideal for transporting fresh or frozen goods
- **Durable material**: Made from robust, highly insulating polyethylene, suitable for intensive use
- **Thermal insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam, free of CFCs and HCFCs, providing excellent thermal performance
- **Internal adapters**: Stainless steel guides designed to accommodate GN containers, ensuring optimal load organization
- **Available models**:
- **Internal dimensions**: Compatible with GN 1/1 containers, allowing for efficient space management
