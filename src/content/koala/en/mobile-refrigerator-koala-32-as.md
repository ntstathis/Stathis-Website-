This **27-liter isothermal container** ensures maximum thermal performance and is ideal for the safe transport of temperature-sensitive products. Made from high-quality polyethylene and insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam, it protects the organoleptic properties of food and reduces the risk of bacterial growth.

- **Capacity**: 27 liters
- **Material**: Durable and insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (free of CFCs and HCFCs)
- **Power supply**: Functional design compatible with dual power sources, 12Vdc and 230Vac
- **User-friendly handling**: Top-opening lid and integrated carrying handles for safe and comfortable transport
