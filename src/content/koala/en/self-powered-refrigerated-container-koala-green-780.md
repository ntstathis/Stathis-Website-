**The Koala Green 780** is a self-powered isothermal refrigerated container designed for the transportation of temperature-controlled products.

- **Capacity**: 610 liters, with a front-opening door for easy and quick access.
- **Self-powered operation**: Equipped with an integrated battery that ensures autonomous operation. The battery can be easily recharged either by connecting the container to a standard 230Vac power supply or by removing it from the refrigeration unit to recharge it with a dedicated external power adapter.
- **Construction materials**: Made of polyethylene and insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam, ensuring high thermal performance.
- **Performance**: Maintains optimal internal temperature, preserving the organoleptic properties of food and reducing the risk of bacterial growth.
- **External dimensions**: 80×100 cm, ideal for flexible use and compatible with various commercial vehicles.
