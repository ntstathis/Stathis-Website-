The **KOALA 70** is an isothermal refrigerated container designed for the transport of temperature-controlled products. With a capacity of 68 liters and GN 1/1 internal dimensions, it ensures excellent thermal retention, preserving the organoleptic properties of food and reducing the risk of bacterial growth. Made from high-quality polyethylene and insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam, it delivers high performance with respect for the environment.

- **Capacity**: 68 liters
- **Material**: Durable and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam, free of CFCs and HCFCs
- **Smart design**: Front-opening door for easy product access
- **Modularity**: Stackable and compatible with multi-temperature transport systems
- **Versatility**: GN 1/1 internal dimensions, suitable for a wide range of applications
