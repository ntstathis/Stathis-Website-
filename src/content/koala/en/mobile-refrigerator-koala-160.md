The **KOALA 160** is a mobile refrigerated container designed for the safe transport and optimal temperature preservation of temperature-sensitive products. Featuring an integrated cooling unit and a top-opening design, it offers ease of use and maximum efficiency.

- Durable polyethylene structure, built for intensive use
- Eco-friendly polyurethane foam insulation, free of CFCs and HCFCs, ensuring excellent thermal performance

- **Internal capacity**: 162 liters, ideal for transporting and storing temperature-controlled goods
- **High-quality materials**:
- **Integrated cooling unit**: Provides consistent and efficient refrigeration
- **Practical design**: Top-opening lid allows for easy loading and unloading
- **Available with wheels**: For enhanced mobility and ease of transport
