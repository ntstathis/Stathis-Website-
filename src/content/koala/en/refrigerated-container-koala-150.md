The **KOALA 150** is an isothermal refrigerated container designed to ensure the safe and professional transport of temperature-controlled products. With a capacity of 148 liters, it is made from high-quality polyethylene and insulated with eco-friendly polyurethane foam free of CFCs and HCFCs. The KOALA 150 delivers excellent thermal performance while being environmentally conscious.

- **Capacity**: 148 liters
- **Material**: Durable and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Functional design**: Front-opening door for easy and quick product access
- **Internal flexibility**: Compatible with GN 1/1 and 600×400 mm trays and containers, using optional rails and adapters available as accessories
