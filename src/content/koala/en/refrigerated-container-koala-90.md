The **KOALA 90** is an isothermal refrigerated container designed to ensure the safe transport of temperature-controlled products. With a 90-liter capacity and GN 1/1 internal dimensions, it guarantees excellent thermal retention, preserving the organoleptic properties of food and minimizing the risk of bacterial growth. Made from high-quality polyethylene and insulated with eco-friendly polyurethane foam free of CFCs and HCFCs, the KOALA 90 combines high performance with environmental responsibility—making it a reliable choice for professional transport.

- **Capacity**: 90 liters
- **Material**: Durable and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Practical design**: Front-opening door for quick and easy access to products
- **Modularity**: Stackable and compatible with multi-temperature transport systems
- **Versatility**: GN 1/1 internal dimensions, ideal for a wide range of applications
