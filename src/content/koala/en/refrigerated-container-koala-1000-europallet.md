The **Koala 1000** is the ideal isothermal refrigerated container for the safe and professional transport of temperature-controlled products.

- **Large capacity**: 830 liters, perfect for transporting high volumes while maintaining optimal temperatures
- **Robust construction**: Made from durable polyethylene and insulated with eco-friendly polyurethane foam, free of CFCs and HCFCs, ensuring excellent thermal retention, preserving the organoleptic properties of food, and reducing the risk of bacterial growth
- **Optimized dimensions**: Compliant with EUROPALLET standards (80×120 cm), allowing efficient use of loading space in utility vehicles and improving overall transport efficiency
- **Advanced security**: Equipped with a stainless steel closing handle with a release button and pre-fitted for a tamper-evident system, ensuring maximum transport security
- **Dual power supply**: Compatible with 12Vdc and 230Vac, offering maximum flexibility for both transit and stationary use
