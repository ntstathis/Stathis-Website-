The **Koala Green 500** refrigerated container is a self-powered, insulated cooling unit, ideal for transporting temperature-sensitive products.

- **Capacity**: 500 liters, with a front-opening door for easy access.
- **Self-powered**: Equipped with an integrated battery that ensures autonomous operation. The battery can be easily recharged by plugging the container into a standard 230Vac outlet or by removing it from the cooling unit and charging it with a dedicated external charger.
- **Construction materials**: Made of polyethylene and insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam to ensure effective thermal retention.
- **Performance**: Maintains internal temperature, preserving the organoleptic properties of food and reducing the risk of bacterial growth.
