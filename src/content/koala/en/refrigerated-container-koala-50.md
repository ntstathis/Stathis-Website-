The **Koala 50** is the ideal mobile refrigerated container for the safe transport of temperature-controlled products.

- **Robust construction**: Made with CFC- and HCFC-free materials, ensuring excellent thermal performance, preserving the organoleptic properties of transported goods, and reducing the risk of bacterial growth.
- **Optimal capacity**: 53 liters, ideal for transporting laboratory samples or other temperature-sensitive items.
- **Integrated handles**: Practical stainless steel handles for secure grip and easy transport.
- **Internal protection**: Equipped with an evaporator protection grid made of plastic-coated metal wire, ensuring safety and long-lasting durability.
- **Practical design**: Top-opening lid for simple and direct access to contents.
- **Dual power supply**: Compatible with 12Vdc and 230Vac, offering flexible use both on the move and in stationary environments.
