The **Koala Green 600** refrigerated container is an innovative solution, fully autonomous thanks to its LiFePO4 battery, making it independent from any vehicle. It is equipped with a removable battery that can be recharged by connecting the container to a 230V power supply or by using an external charger after removing it from the cooling unit.

- **Capacity**: 600 liters, ideal for transporting temperature-controlled products.
- **Dual power supply**: Compatible with both 12Vdc and 230Vac power sources for enhanced flexibility.
- **Construction materials**: Made of polyethylene and insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam, ensuring excellent thermal retention and preservation of food’s organoleptic properties.
- **Performance**: Guarantees optimal temperature maintenance, reducing the risk of bacterial growth.
- **Integrated cooling unit**: Designed for high performance and ease of use.
