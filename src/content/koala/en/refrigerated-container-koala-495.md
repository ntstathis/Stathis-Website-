The **KOALA 495** is an isothermal refrigerated container with an integrated cooling unit, ATP certification, and a capacity of 495 liters. Made from high-quality polyethylene and insulated with eco-friendly polyurethane foam free of CFCs and HCFCs, the KOALA 495 delivers excellent thermal performance combined with environmental sustainability. Its internal dimensions (600×400 mm) make it ideal for transporting boxes and trays, maximizing loading space in utility vehicles. Compliant with FD X 15-140 standards, it offers a reliable and flexible solution for professional transport needs.

- **Capacity**: 495 liters
- **Material**: Durable and highly insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Certification**: ATP certified and compliant with FD X 15-140 standards
- **Practical design**: Front-opening door for easy product access
- **Internal flexibility**: 600×400 mm dimensions, ideal for boxes and trays
- **Space optimization**: Designed to maximize loading efficiency in utility vehicles
