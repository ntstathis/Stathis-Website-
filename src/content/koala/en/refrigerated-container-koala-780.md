Refrigerated isothermal container designed for the transport of temperature-controlled products, featuring the following specifications:

- **Capacity**: 610 liters
- **Front-opening door**: For convenient and quick access
- **Materials**: Made of polyethylene, insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam
- **Thermal insulation**: Ensures optimal temperature retention, preserves the organoleptic properties of food, and reduces the risk of bacterial growth
- **External dimensions**: 80×100 cm, compatible with various commercial vehicles
- **Available versions**: With cooling unit mounted either on the top or at the rear, tailored to specific transport needs
