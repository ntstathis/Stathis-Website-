Ideal for the safe transport of temperature-controlled products, this **73-liter isothermal container** delivers maximum thermal performance. Made from high-quality polyethylene and insulated with CFC- and HCFC-free polyurethane foam, it preserves the organoleptic properties of food and reduces the risk of bacterial growth.

- **Capacity**: 73 liters
- **Internal dimensions**: GN 1/1
- **Material**: Durable and insulating polyethylene
- **Insulation**: Eco-friendly polyurethane foam (CFC- and HCFC-free)
- **Power supply**: Compatible with dual power sources, 12Vdc and 230Vac
- **Functional design**: Top-opening lid for safe and comfortable handling
