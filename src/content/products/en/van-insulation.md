    <div class="black_box bg-black text-white radius-24-16 overflow-hidden d-flex flex-column justify-content-center">
      <p class="ff-pfdin-bold fs-md-32 fs-20 mb-20">Construction of Insulation KIT</p>
      <p>The insulation of the loading area is made with the most modern production methods:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Detailed 3D scanning of the vehicle's cargo space</li>
        <li>Panel development by CNC machines (roof, side walls, front wall and floor)</li>
        <li>Polyester molds construction for the doors and doors’ frame</li>
        <li>3D pieces construction where needed (handles etc)</li>
      </ul>
      <p>Panels consist of reinforced polyester and special type extruded polystyrene, that has improved mechanical characteristics and excellent moisture behavior.</p>
      <p>The panels cover all the nerves of the body, so there are no thermal bridges at all.</p>
    </div>

<div class="row mb-30">

    <div class="black_box bg-black text-white radius-24-16 overflow-hidden d-flex flex-column justify-content-center">
      <p class="ff-pfdin-bold fs-md-32 fs-20 mb-20">The insulation on the rear doors of the Van</p>
      <p>There is also insulation on the doors, using special molds that can be removed, to allow any repairs needed (change of lock, hinges, etc.).</p>
      <p>A second frame is created at the door openings, in order to achieve the best thermal permeability coefficients.</p>
      <p>In difficult places of the loading area (such as locks, door support points, etc.), special pieces are manufactured by 3D printers, for a better fit with the rest of the panels.</p>
    </div>

    <h3>Floor</h3>
    <p>The floor is made of reinforced polyester panel with marine wood, it has a non-slip surface and high resistance that makes it suitable for demanding loading conditions.</p>
    <p>At the lower part of the doors, are placed special stainless parts to protect the floor from the loads. Also, a drainage hole is opened on the floor for the removal of the liquids.</p>
