## The Main Characteristics

The main features of the system in its basic version are as follows:

- Operating voltage of the recorder 10V to 36V DC
- Temperature measurement range from -40o C to +50o C
- Possibility of setting and displaying the measurements every 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes (setting every 15 minutes is recommended)
- Two temperature measurement sensors, one at the front, at the air intake of the machine, and one near the chamber doors
- Built-in thermal printer with the possibility of printing delivery ticket and journey ticket in numerical or graphic form.
- Memory for more than two years of data (in the standard version with two sensors and recordings every 15 minutes)
- Protection via PIN codes of the central menu for changing thermograph parameters
- Infrared communication port for data transfer to PC
- Alarm in case of deviation of temperatures from the preset temperature, and possibility of multiple programming of alarms

## Optional extra equipment:

- Four additional sensors for recording temperatures.
- Sensors for recording humidity
- Sensors for door openings, machine operation, and defrosting.
