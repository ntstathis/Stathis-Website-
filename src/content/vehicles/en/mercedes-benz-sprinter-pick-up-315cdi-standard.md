## Warranty

A) Coverage from day one.

All new Mercedes-Benz vehicles come with a **two-year new vehicle warranty**. This warranty is provided in addition to the legal liability for material defects and offers peace of mind during the first two years.

The warranty is valid from the date of vehicle delivery or the date of first registration, whichever comes first, with **no mileage limitation**.

The contractual warranty is provided by the **Authorized Dealer** who carries out the sale. It is recognized in all member states of the European Union, as well as in **Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and Switzerland**.

B) **The Electric Vehicle Warranty**

Electric vehicles come with a **2-year warranty** with **no mileage limitation**, covering both mechanical and electrical components (e.g. suspension, steering system, switches, infotainment, etc.).

In addition to the standard 2-year warranty, an **electric battery certificate** is also provided. This ensures warranty coverage through **replacement or repair of the high-voltage battery** in the event of malfunction for a period of **8 years or 160,000 km**—whichever comes first—or if the battery’s maximum capacity falls below **70% of its original capacity**.
